Artifact Reflection

I selected this artifact because it demonstrates both my technical knowledge and my ability to present information visually. While developing the timeline, I learned that the growth of artificial intelligence did not occur in a straight line. The field experienced periods of progress, disappointment, reduced funding, and renewed innovation.

Creating the artifact also improved my research, web-design, and communication skills. This project is relevant to my professional goals because understanding the evolution of AI helps me evaluate current technologies more thoughtfully.
